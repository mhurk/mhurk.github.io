Home battery data

This is some data of my AlphaESS home battery. I choose a 9.3 kWh battery as I think it's a good balance between cost and performance, higher capacity batteries do yield a bit more but that is mainly during summer months when there is enough solar energy and the consumption is limited. These graphs show the state of charge over time.

Solar energy data

This spider plot (or radar plot if you have a mild form of arachnophobia) shows the energy produced by my 12 solar panels over different months and years. The other chart shows inverter temperature versus AC output power, with every Throttled (power-capped) sample picked out against the general MPPT density — Throttled sits at a fairly fixed power level (~3700 W) across a range of temperatures, so it reads as a power ceiling rather than a thermal cutoff. This matches the max of my 16A fuse. More info on MPPT on Wikipedia
